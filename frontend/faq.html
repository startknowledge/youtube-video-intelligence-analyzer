Frequently Asked Questions

What is YouTube Video Intelligence Analyzer?

It is a web tool that analyzes supplied YouTube video information and produces structured creator-focused insights such as summaries, keywords, SEO observations, content gaps, FAQs, title ideas, and related content concepts.

Does it show private YouTube analytics?

No. A public video URL does not automatically provide private Studio metrics such as private revenue, private CTR, private watch time, or private audience demographics.

Can it analyze a transcript?

Yes, when transcript data is available to the application and supplied to the AI analysis layer. Transcript-aware analysis can improve understanding of the actual spoken content.

Is the transcript always available?

No. Transcript availability depends on the video, language, captions, permissions, platform behavior, and the application's supported retrieval method.

Are keyword scores official YouTube scores?

No. Any keyword importance or opportunity score generated by the analyzer is an analytical recommendation, not an official YouTube ranking score.

Can it guarantee ranking?

No. YouTube ranking depends on many factors that are not fully observable through public metadata. No third-party analyzer can honestly guarantee a particular position, number of views, CTR, or revenue.

What information is analyzed?

Depending on availability, the application can analyze title, channel, category, publication date, duration, description, tags, public statistics, chapters, keywords, SEO analysis, description structure, and transcript.

Why did my analysis fail?

Possible causes include an invalid URL, temporary API failure, unavailable video information, provider rate limits, transcript unavailability, network problems, or an AI provider returning an invalid response.

Why does the result say “Not available”?

The analyzer is designed not to invent missing information. If the application does not receive a field, the responsible result is to identify it as unavailable.

Can I analyze Shorts?

If the application's URL parser and supported metadata workflow accept the Shorts URL, the same general analysis approach can be used. Results depend on the information available for that video.

Can I use the results for my own YouTube channel?

Yes, as a planning and research aid. You should independently verify important facts and decide whether a recommendation fits your audience, brand, and content strategy.

Can I copy another creator's content?

No. Analysis does not grant permission to copy copyrighted video content, scripts, graphics, music, or branding. Use the insights for research and create original work while respecting applicable rights.

Does the analyzer store videos?

The analyzer is intended to process information for analysis. Storage and retention can depend on the current implementation and infrastructure. Review the Privacy Policy for the applicable information-handling approach.

What is content gap analysis?

Content gaps are opportunities suggested by comparing the supplied topic, explanation, metadata, transcript, and related questions. They are recommendations rather than proof that no other creator has covered a subject.

What are CTA ideas?

CTA means call to action. The analyzer can suggest ways to encourage an audience to watch another video, subscribe, comment, visit a resource, or take another relevant action.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the Contact or Support page. Include the error and relevant public video URL, but never send passwords or API keys.
