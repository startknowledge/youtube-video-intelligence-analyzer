Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Video Intelligence Analyzer handles information when you visit or use the website. We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, improve, and support the service.

1. Information you provide

You may provide information when you submit a YouTube URL, contact support, send feedback, or otherwise communicate with the service. A submitted video URL may be processed so that the application can identify the requested video and retrieve or analyze information that is available to the application. If you voluntarily send an email address or other contact details through a support form, that information may be used to respond to your request.

2. YouTube video information

The analyzer may process publicly available video information such as video ID, title, channel title, category, publication date, duration, description, tags where available, public statistics where permitted, chapters, and transcript data where a transcript is available and provided through the application's supported workflow. The service does not intentionally claim access to private YouTube Studio information merely because a public video URL has been submitted.

3. AI processing

Some analysis features may send supplied video information to an AI provider configured by the application. This can include metadata, descriptions, keywords, chapters, and transcript text when transcript analysis is enabled. The purpose is to generate summaries, content ideas, SEO observations, FAQs, title suggestions, and other requested intelligence. The service should not send passwords, private channel credentials, or secret account information as part of ordinary public-video analysis.

4. Technical information

Like most websites, the service may receive technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps, referrer information, and error information through hosting, security, logging, or analytics systems. Such information can be used for security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and aggregate usage analysis.

5. Cookies

The website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality, security, preferences, analytics, or other clearly described purposes. Optional analytics or advertising technologies, if enabled, may be subject to their own provider policies and applicable consent requirements. See the Cookies Policy for more information.

6. Data retention

Retention depends on the purpose for which information was collected. Temporary processing data may be retained only for the period needed to complete an analysis or diagnose a problem. Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain service records. Security logs may be retained according to operational and security requirements.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or loss. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid submitting passwords, API keys, private credentials, or other secrets through public forms.

8. Third-party services

The website may rely on third-party infrastructure for hosting, AI processing, analytics, security, email, or other technical functions. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the policies of third-party services when their features are used.

9. External links

The website may contain links to YouTube, StartKnowledge, documentation, social networks, or other external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external services. Review the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing information.

10. Children's privacy

The service is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for unnecessary purposes. If you believe a child has submitted personal information that should be removed, contact the service operator with sufficient details to investigate the request.

11. Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Requests should be sent through the Contact or Support page. We may need enough information to verify a request before acting on it.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service changes, new technologies are introduced, or legal requirements evolve. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, use the website's Contact or Support page. Please do not include passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, or other sensitive credentials in your message.
