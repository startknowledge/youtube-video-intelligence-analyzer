Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how YouTube Video Intelligence Analyzer may use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, or other mechanisms used for functionality, security, analytics, or preferences.

Essential cookies

Essential technologies may be required for basic website operation, security, session handling, routing, load balancing, or fraud prevention. These technologies generally cannot be disabled through a normal website preference control without affecting functionality.

Preference technologies

The site may use local storage or cookies to remember settings such as interface preferences, dismissed notices, or other choices. These technologies are used to improve the experience and reduce repeated configuration.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, they may collect aggregate information about pages viewed, interactions, device type, browser, approximate location derived by the provider, referral information, and performance events. Analytics settings may change as the website evolves.

Security

Security systems may use cookies or related signals to identify suspicious traffic, protect endpoints, limit abuse, and maintain service reliability.

Third-party services

Embedded or linked third-party services may set their own cookies. Examples can include social media widgets, analytics platforms, video services, or external resources. Those services operate under their own policies.

How to control cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to block, delete, or restrict cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies can prevent some features from working correctly. Browser controls vary by product and version.

Changes

This policy may be updated when technologies or website functionality change. The revised date will be displayed at the top of the page.

Privacy

Cookie information is part of the broader privacy practices described in the Privacy Policy. If you have a question about a particular cookie or tracking technology, contact the service through the Contact or Support page.
